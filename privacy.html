Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AguTech Labs ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through agutechlabs.com, how we use it, and your rights.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and the message you send us.
  • Email: any information you include when you email us directly.
  • Theme preference: your light or dark mode choice is stored in your own browser and is never sent to us.

How we use your information

We only use your information to reply to your enquiry, provide quotes and deliver the services you request. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

  • Form processing: messages sent through our contact form may be processed by Formspree, which delivers them to our inbox.
  • Fonts: this site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.
  • Hosting: our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and performance.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed to respond and manage any resulting project, or as required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing info@agutechlabs.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

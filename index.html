FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Send me a message.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A landing page usually takes 1–2 weeks and a business website 3–6 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is quoted based on the pages, features and content you need. After a short call I'll send you a fixed, itemised quote, so there are no surprises.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your goals, any branding you have (logo, colours, fonts), the text and images for your site, and examples of websites you like. If you don't have content yet, I can help you plan it.

Will my website work on phones and tablets?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first and tested on real phones, tablets and desktops across modern browsers.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. I can build on a platform or CMS that lets you edit text and images yourself, and I'll show you how to do it before handover.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a support period after launch for fixes and small changes, and ongoing maintenance is available if you need it.
